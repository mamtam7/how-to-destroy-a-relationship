# How to Destroy a Relationship
### A Practical Manual, Illustrated by Real Events

---

## Concept

This is not a self-help book. It is an autopsy.

Most books about relationships tell you what to do right. This one documents what was done wrong — systematically, repeatedly, and often without awareness — and names the psychological mechanics underneath each behavior.

The source material is a real relationship: seven years of messages, arguments, caregiving, love, and failure. The names and identifying details have been changed. The events are documented. The patterns are named. The research explains why they work so effectively as instruments of destruction.

If you recognize yourself in these pages — as the one doing it, or the one it was done to — that's the point.

---

## Format

Each chapter covers one **method of destruction**. The structure is:

1. **The Method** — What it looks like, in plain language
2. **In Practice** — Real examples from the source record, quoted directly
3. **The Psychology** — What research says about why it works, what it does to the target, and why it's so hard to name in the moment
4. **The Damage Report** — The long-term cost to the relationship
5. **The Irony** — What the person doing it usually believed they were doing instead

## Source Material

Primary: Seven years of iMessage records between two people who loved each other and couldn't figure out how to stay.

Secondary: Psychology research on the mechanisms documented in each chapter.

---

## Tone

Not angry. Not vindictive. Honest.

The goal is not to assign blame — it is to name what happened clearly enough that someone else might recognize it before it's too late.
